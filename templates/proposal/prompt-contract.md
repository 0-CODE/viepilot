# Proposal Prompt Contract

## Voice
The maker's first-person voice ("tôi"/"chúng tôi", "I"/"we") is allowed and expected. Every claim of pride must carry evidence: a number, customer reference, or demo. No unbacked absolutes like "tốt nhất", "đột phá", "revolutionary", or "best-in-class".

## Rhythm
Mix short and long sentences for natural flow. Never more than 2 consecutive bullets with the same grammatical structure. A slide may carry a single large sentence instead of bullets. Removes the "8–15 words, action verb" formula.

## Narrative arc
Structure every deck along this arc: context → conflict → turning point → solution → proof → invitation. Map this to the slide sequence.

## Real material
Never invent anecdotes, customers, or numbers. When the Story Brief is empty, write `[cần chất liệu thật]` / `[needs real material]` in place of generic claims. Prompt the user to provide the truth.

## Numbers
Keep digits and symbols (`96%`, `3x`, `$50K`). Speaker notes are full spoken words in spoken style, but numbers stay as digits — never spell numbers out in words.

## Banned clichés
The following phrases and their equivalents are blacklisted. Check all AI-generated text against this list and remove them:
- Vi: "Trong thời đại số", "Không chỉ… mà còn", "Hãy cùng khám phá", "giải pháp toàn diện", "nâng tầm", "đột phá", "tối ưu hoá trải nghiệm", "hệ sinh thái", "trong bối cảnh hiện nay", "đóng vai trò quan trọng", "mang lại giá trị", "không ngừng nỗ lực"
- En: "In today's fast-paced world", "leverage", "seamless", "unlock the power", "cutting-edge", "game-changer", "revolutionize", "synergy", "best-in-class", "delve", "in conclusion", "it is important to note"
