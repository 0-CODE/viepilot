## Details

### Free-text purpose consult (runs before the ENH-119 audience intake)
- Step opens with one free-text prompt: purpose, context, who will be in the room, what outcome is wanted.
- AI produces a short consult in the user's language:
  - "What I understood" (3–5 lines, checkable by the user)
  - Recommended type + variant with why, plus 1–2 alternatives
  - Suggested structure outline (beats) and duration
  - Watch-outs specific to this audience (e.g. council reviewers want methodology before results)
  - What is still missing → maps onto the 4 intake groups (roles, decision maker, knowledge/size, format/goal)
- Then AUQ asks ONLY the missing groups; the user confirms or corrects the understanding and the recommendation.
- Deterministic helpers back the LLM step: hint extraction from text (vi + en keywords) and gap detection, so missing groups are computed, not guessed.

### `research-proposal` type — variants
- `grant-proposal` — research funding application (urgency/rationale, state of the art, objectives, methodology, expected outputs, team, budget, timeline)
- `kickoff` — project/topic kickoff briefing (thuyết minh kickoff đề tài, dự án khoa học): goals, scope, work packages, roles, milestones, risks, reporting cadence
- `progress-report` — mid-term progress or final acceptance (nghiệm thu): results vs objectives, outputs/publications, deviations, budget use, next steps
- `defense` — thesis defense / conference talk: question, contribution, method, results, limitations, future work
- `tech-transfer` — research results to industry / R&D commercialization: problem in industry terms, technology readiness, evidence, IP, partnership model, pilot plan

### Academic personas + research criteria
- New personas (scope `research`): scientific council reviewer, funding agency program officer, peer scientist in the field, industry partner evaluating transfer, PhD student / junior researcher.
- Research-only rubric criteria (applied only for `research-proposal`): novelty/contribution, methodological rigor, feasibility & reproducibility. Existing H1–H2, C1–C8 and blockers unchanged; an extra blocker for unsupported scientific claims / missing citation for a key claim.
