## Summary
Proposals keep cramming in information to look honest: what is not done yet, things to watch,
"remaining" items, known limitations. These are development notes, not content for the audience.
Add an always-on persona — the **Backstage Editor** — whose only job is to find disadvantageous and
dev-note content, so each item is fixed, reframed, moved backstage (presenter-only), or removed.

## Principle: move it backstage, never lie
- Non-material dev notes (TODOs, "chưa làm", "còn lại", internal concerns, WIP details, known minor
  issues) do not belong on slides → move to presenter-only **Backstage notes** (Q&A prep) or drop.
- **Material** items — anything that changes the audience's decision (budget overrun, schedule slip,
  security/legal exposure, a promised capability that does not exist) — may NOT be hidden from a
  decision audience. They are reframed as **risk → mitigation → ask** on a slide, or kept as a
  prepared answer when the audience is not the decision maker. The existing "false claim" and
  "over-reaching legal/security promise" blockers stay in force.
- Never invent progress to cover a gap; if a slide's claim depends on unfinished work, the claim is
  softened or the slide is changed.

## Details

### Persona #31 — Backstage Editor (Biên tập viên Hậu trường), always on
- Runs in every council review (like #20 Humanity editor) and in the Rehearsal Hall (ENH-121) as an
  observer, not an audience member (does not count in quick-mode 6–8, does not affect retention).
- Scans slides AND speaker notes AND script for:
  - unfinished / not-done work ("chưa làm", "chưa hoàn thành", "đang làm", "sẽ làm sau", "TODO", "WIP", "pending", "not yet", "planned but")
  - "things to watch / remaining" lists ("cần quan tâm", "lưu ý", "còn lại", "tồn đọng", "known issues", "limitations", "caveats")
  - self-undermining hedges and over-disclosure ("tạm thời", "chưa ổn định", "hy vọng", "chưa chắc", "workaround", "hack")
  - internal process detail that belongs in a dev log (overlaps ENH-122 process-leak guard)
- For each finding outputs `{slide, location: slide|notes|script, quote, kind: dev-note|weakness|risk, material: bool, why, action}` with action ∈
  - `fix` — the gap can be closed before presenting (tell the user what to do)
  - `reframe` — say it as plan/next step with owner + date, or as risk → mitigation → ask
  - `backstage` — move to presenter-only notes / Q&A prep (non-material only)
  - `remove` — irrelevant to this audience
- `material: true` items can only get `fix` or `reframe` for decision audiences (leadership-report,
  progress-report, grant-proposal, project-proposal approvals); the orchestrator enforces this.

### Deterministic detector (code)
- `detectDevNotes(manifest, script, {lang})` — bilingual phrase tables (vi with/without diacritics,
  en), whole-word matching; returns candidates for the persona and a hard count for the gate.
- Gate: no unresolved dev-note candidates on slides before rendering; candidates in notes must be
  either reframed or moved to the Backstage section.

### Outputs
- `backstage.md` next to the proposal: presenter-only list grouped by slide —
  likely tough questions, honest prepared answers, open items with owner/date, and what was removed
  and why. Never rendered into the .pptx slides; never read aloud in the spoken script.
- Speaker notes keep a short `[Backstage]` pointer only when a question is likely on that slide.
- The council report gets a "Disadvantage scan" section: counts by action, material items and how
  they were handled.
- The user confirms the plan via AskUserQuestion (accept all / review material items one by one / restore an item to the slides).

### Integration
- ENH-122 Content Map gains a category `BACKSTAGE` next to FIT / TRANSFORM / DROP / SENSITIVE.
- ENH-121 Rehearsal Hall: the Backstage Editor reports which removed items listeners asked about
  anyway (signals that something was hidden that the audience actually needed).
